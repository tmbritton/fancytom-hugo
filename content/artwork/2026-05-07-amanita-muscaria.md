The amanita muscaria mushroom, also called the "Christmas Mushroom", is an iconic mushroom with a red cap and white dots shaped like Frosted Flakes. It's a standard of stoner art even though it contains a different psychoactive compound than your standard psilocybin mushrooms. I've read that indigenous people of northern Europe would drink the urine of reindeer who had eaten this mushroom to get the intoxicating effects, since the mushroom itself is poisonous for humans to consume directly. Given the red-and-white coloring and the reindeer connection, that's where the association with Christmas comes from.
