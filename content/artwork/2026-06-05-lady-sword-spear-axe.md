This is a very Frazetta-influenced artwork. It's hand-drawn and inked, then scanned, cleaned up and digitally colored. I call it Lady Sword Spear Axe. She's a topless barbarian that roams the mountains doing whatever the fuck she wants, which is mostly fighting and drinking. Her sword-spear-axe was created by Dwarven blacksmiths as a joke and she began wielding it as a drunken bet. She found she liked the heft. It made it quite efficient to perform decapitations at a 6-foot distance.
